# Lab 4 Instructor Notes: Planning Pattern

## Assessment Targets

- At least 85% of students produce an initial plan with scope, evidence collection, comparison, and decision phases.
- At least 80% of submissions execute plan steps logically and record observation updates after major steps.
- At least 80% of submissions perform an observation-driven replan when contradictory evidence appears.
- At least 85% of final submissions reconstruct at least 90% of key timeline events.
- At least 80% of final submissions correctly classify whether key events occurred inside, outside, or unresolved within the incident window.
- At least 80% of submissions explain one planning-agent suggestion that required evidence-driven revision.

## Assessment Method

Score the planning log, observation log, and final report with a shared 0–4 rubric for plan completeness, step order, replanning quality, diagnosis of planning-agent limits, and conclusion support. Aggregate scores to evaluate the targets.

When staffing permits, double-score a subset of submissions and reconcile differences with a shared scoring guide. Record target attainment, timeline-reconstruction accuracy, and common issues such as missing dependencies, uncritical acceptance of agent plans, missing replanning, and unsupported conclusions.
