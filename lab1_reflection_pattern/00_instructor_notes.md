# Lab 1 Instructor Notes: Reflection Pattern

## Assessment Targets

- At least 80% of final-report claims map to explicit artifact evidence.
- Final reports contain zero major unsupported conclusions.
- At least 85% of timeline events in the final report are correctly ordered and timestamped.
- Draft-to-revision rubric scores improve by at least 20% on average per cohort.

## Assessment Method

Score the model draft, revised model draft, and student evidence-check table with a shared 0–4 rubric for claim-to-evidence links, reasoning validity, timeline accuracy, and uncertainty handling. Compute draft-to-revision gain per submission and aggregate the results for the cohort.

When staffing permits, double-score a subset of submissions, reconcile differences with a shared scoring guide, and record target-attainment rates and average improvement.
