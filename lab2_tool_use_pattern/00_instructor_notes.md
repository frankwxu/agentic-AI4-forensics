# Lab 2 Instructor Notes: Tool Use Pattern

## Assessment Targets

- At least 85% of students execute `list_media_files` -> `inspect_image_evidence` -> `inspect_listing_records` in logical order.
- At least 90% of submitted tool calls use valid arguments and required formatting.
- At least 80% of final submissions correctly classify the evidence as confirmed, likely, or unconfirmed online-sale preparation.
- At least 85% of final submissions include specific file or record references for each major claim.
- At least 80% of final submissions identify the strongest timestamp and vehicle-match evidence.
- At least 80% of final submissions justify one tool-calling suggestion they accept, correct, or reject.

## Assessment Method

Score the tool-call log and final report with a shared 0–4 rubric for tool selection, argument validity, output interpretation, evaluation of agent suggestions, and conclusion support. A correct sequence performs the core calls before the final conclusion; additional justified calls are allowed.

When staffing permits, double-score a subset of submissions and reconcile differences with a shared scoring guide. Record target attainment, timestamp-and-vehicle-match accuracy, and common issues such as invalid arguments, uncritical acceptance of agent suggestions, missing evidence references, and overclaiming.
