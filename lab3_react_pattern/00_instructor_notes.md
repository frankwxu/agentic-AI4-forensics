# Lab 3 Instructor Notes: ReAct Pattern

## Assessment Targets

- At least 85% of students correctly describe the four ReAct stages.
- At least 80% of submissions use tool calls relevant to the forensic question.
- At least 80% of submissions use an observation from one step to justify the next.
- At least 85% of final submissions answer the communication-timing question with evidence-cited reasoning.
- At least 80% of final submissions avoid claiming confirmed delivery when artifacts show only an attempt or incomplete network evidence.

## Assessment Method

Score the ReAct step log, tool-call justification notes, and final answer with a shared 0–4 rubric for loop understanding, relevance of tool choice, observation use, conclusion support, and explanation of uncertainty. Aggregate scores to evaluate the targets.
