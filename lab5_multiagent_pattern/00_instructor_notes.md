# Lab 5 Instructor Notes: Multiagent Pattern

## Assessment Targets

- At least 85% of students use all three required roles with role-appropriate prompts.
- At least 80% of submissions identify and resolve at least one conflict between role outputs.
- At least 85% of final submissions include a complete evidence-handling timeline with required transfer checks.
- At least 80% of final submissions correctly classify the suspected file transmission as confirmed, likely, or unconfirmed.
- At least 85% of final submissions cite files or logs and the evidence-handling record for each major claim.
- At least 80% of submissions diagnose at least one agent disagreement or failure mode using role definitions and evidence.

## Assessment Method

Score the multiagent coordination log, conflict-resolution notes, and final report with a shared 0–4 rubric for role assignment, cross-agent checking, diagnosis of disagreement, evidence-handling analysis, and conclusion support. Aggregate scores to evaluate the targets.

When staffing permits, double-score a subset of submissions and reconcile differences with a shared scoring guide. Record target attainment, evidence-handling-gap accuracy, and common issues such as role confusion, unresolved conflicts, unexplained record gaps, and unsupported conclusions.
