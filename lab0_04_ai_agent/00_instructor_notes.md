# Lab 0 Instructor Notes: Bounded AI-Agent Workflow

## Assessment Targets

- At least 85% of students correctly identify the role and goal, approved tools, validation, working memory, stop condition, structured result, and human-review boundary in the forensic walkthrough.
- At least 80% of submissions correctly explain how the program prevents an unapproved or already-used tool request from running.
- At least 80% of submissions accurately trace which approved tool results enter working memory and how that information is available on a later turn.
- At least 85% of final reviews distinguish supported device activity from information that remains unknown.
- At least 85% of final reviews avoid claiming who acted, what the screenshot contained, confirmed delivery, or misconduct when the available records do not establish those facts.

## Assessment Method

Score the completed `07_forensic_agent_walkthrough.ipynb` and its reflection responses with a shared 0–4 rubric for bounded-agent component mapping, tool-request validation, working-memory traceability, evidence-bounded observations, and uncertainty and human-review handling. Use the printed tool trace, working memory, and final structured review as evidence when scoring the reflection.

Aggregate scores to record target attainment and common issues, such as treating the LLM as the entire agent, confusing a tool request with a tool result, overlooking program validation, or overclaiming from the synthetic case packet. When staffing permits, double-score a subset of submissions and reconcile differences with a shared scoring guide.
